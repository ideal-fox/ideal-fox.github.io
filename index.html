ChargeProof

Is your charger lying to you? Plug in a charger, enter the wattage printed on it, and ChargeProof tells you in 30 seconds whether it really delivers that power — in plain words, not just a wall of numbers.

What it does

  • Charger and cable test: measures the power actually delivered and compares it with the wattage on the label. Tells you if it matches, runs below spec, or is probably not what it claims.
  • Saved tests, ranked: keep your results and see which combination is fastest, so you can find the weak cable instead of guessing.
  • Battery care: honest capacity estimates, clearly labelled as estimates, plus a charge-limit alert.
  • No account, everything on your phone. Measurements never leave the device.

Honest by design

Some phones do not expose charging current, and phones deliberately slow down near full charge. When the app cannot judge something, it says so instead of inventing a number.
