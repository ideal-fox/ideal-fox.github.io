ChargeProof – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

ChargeProof is a charger and cable testing utility. Plug in a charger, enter the wattage printed on it, and the app tells you whether it really delivers that power. This policy explains what data is handled and why. Your measurements are processed on your device and are never uploaded to us.

App: ChargeProof (com.jettfox.chargeproof) · Developer: JettFox · Contact: jettfox.designed@gmail.com

1. What we collect

  • Battery and device data (stored on your device only): battery level, voltage, current, temperature, charging state, inferred charging protocol, and your charge/discharge session history. This stays in the app's private storage and is never sent to us.
  • Purchase data: purchases are processed by Google Play Billing and managed through RevenueCat. We receive and store an anonymous purchase identifier and your entitlement status (for example, whether PRO is active) so we can unlock features and restore purchases. Card details are handled by Google Play; we never see or store them.
  • Anonymous identifier: RevenueCat generates a locally stored anonymous ID that links your purchases to your entitlements. It contains no name, email, or other personal information.
  • Advertising identifier: the free version is supported by ads served through Google AdMob. The AdMob SDK processes your device's advertising ID to serve and measure ads. Before an ad is requested in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, the app shows a consent prompt and ads are served only according to your choice. PRO removes ads entirely, and the app makes no ad requests while PRO is active.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, messages, call logs, or your name. There is no account and no login. We never upload your battery or charging measurements.

2. How data is used

  • On your device: charger and cable tests, battery health estimates, statistics and history, charge-limit alerts.
  • Subscriptions: unlocking PRO features and restoring purchases.
  • Advertising: showing the ads that support the free version (never while PRO is active).

4. Subscriptions and purchases

  • PRO Lifetime: a one-time purchase. It does not renew and it is yours to keep.
  • PRO yearly / monthly: auto-renewing subscriptions. You can cancel any time in Google Play > Subscriptions; access continues until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • Restore purchases: available after reinstalling or switching devices, using the same Google account.
  • Refunds follow Google Play policy.

5. Data deletion

“Clear history” in the app deletes your local sessions and saved tests. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything stored on the device. We hold no server-side personal data, because there is no account system.

6. Children

This app is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
